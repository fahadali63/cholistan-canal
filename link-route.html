Flow along the route

Figures are Federal Flood Commission barrage discharges for 20 February, 22 April and 9 September 2025, the three days shown in Figure 5 of the paper. Surplus is flow below Kotri above a delta minimum of 5,000 cusecs (141.6 m³/s), the figure cited by Sindh, since the 1991 Accord leaves it undefined. Whatever the canal draws beyond that surplus is counted as taken from Sindh's supplies and the delta. The planned link upgrade adds up to 5,000 cusecs of capacity to Sulemanki. 1 cusec = 0.0283 m³/s.

Paper: Rafique, S., Ali, F. & Ali, R. B. Concrete before water: governance gaps behind Pakistan's stalled Cholistan Canal and lessons for desert irrigation megaprojects. Manuscript prepared for Water Policy. For the full year-by-year water balance, open the simulator.

Map positions of rivers, barrages and link canals are approximate. The canal and branch routes are schematic, as no alignment drawing is public. The border is drawn only for orientation.
